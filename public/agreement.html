Services Provided
Pola Clip agrees to deliver short-form video clips created from long-form video content provided by the Client, optimized for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and TikTok with captions and client branding applied.

Delivery Timeline
  • Standard delivery: All clips will be delivered within 48 hours of Pola Clip receiving the source video
  • Revision delivery: Revised clips will be delivered within 24 hours of receiving revision notes
  • Delivery is made via Google Drive or email link sent to the Client's registered email address
3
Payment Terms
  • Month 1: 100% of the monthly package fee is due in full before any work begins
  • Month 2 onward: Invoice issued on the 1st of each month, payment due within 15 days (Net-15)
  • Accepted payment methods: Binance Pay, Payoneer, Crypto, Wise
  • Refund policy: No refunds are issued after clips have been delivered and approved
4
Intellectual Property
  • The Client retains full ownership of all original video content and completed clips
  • Pola Clip retains the right to display completed clips as portfolio samples unless the Client requests otherwise in writing
  • The Client confirms they have all necessary rights and permissions for all content in their videos
5
Termination
  • Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice via email
  • Upon termination, all outstanding invoices must be paid in full
  • Termination does not affect the Client's ownership of previously delivered clips
