Services Provided
Pola Clip, a service of Pola IT, agrees to deliver the following on a monthly basis as per the package selected above:
  • Creation of short-form video clips from long-form video content provided by the Client
  • Vertical format (9:16 aspect ratio) optimized for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and TikTok
  • Captions burned directly into all clips (non-removable, permanently embedded)
  • Client branding applied to each clip (logo, channel handle, intro/outro) as per brand kit provided
  • Pro Package only: Custom thumbnail images for YouTube or Facebook

Delivery Timeline & Guarantee
  • Standard delivery: All clips will be delivered within 48 hours of Pola Clip receiving the source video
  • Revision delivery: Revised clips will be delivered within 24 hours of receiving the Client's written revision notes
  • Delivery is made via Google Drive download link sent to the Client's registered email address
  • Delivery timelines begin only after the source video is fully accessible and confirmed received by Pola Clip
  • Pola Clip will notify the Client immediately if a delivery timeline cannot be met due to unforeseen circumstances

  • Revisions are limited to adjustments of existing footage: timing changes, caption corrections, branding adjustments, and cut modifications

  • Revision requests must be submitted in writing (email or message) with specific, clear instructions

Client Responsibilities
The Client agrees to:
  • Provide source video files or accessible video links promptly at the start of each month or upon agreement
  • Provide a complete brand kit (logo file in PNG with transparent background, brand colors, social media handles) within 48 hours of onboarding
  • Specify the number of clips desired per video and any specific moments or focus areas
  • Submit all revision requests in writing with clear, specific instructions
  • Ensure all video links shared are publicly accessible or set to "Anyone with link can view"
  • Confirm that they own or have full rights to all video content shared with Pola Clip

Payment Terms
  • Month 1: 100% of the monthly package fee is due in full before any work begins
  • Month 2 onward: Invoice issued on the 1st of each month, payment due within 15 days (Net-15)
  • Late payment: All work pauses if payment is not received by the due date. Work resumes upon receipt of payment. No penalty fee is charged for the first late payment.
  • Accepted payment methods: Binance Pay, Payoneer
  • Refund policy: No refunds are issued after clips have been delivered and approved. If the Client is genuinely unsatisfied with delivered clips, Pola Clip will provide additional clips at no charge as goodwill resolution, at Pola Clip's discretion.
