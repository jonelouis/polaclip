How It Works

A simple, transparent process from start to finish.

1

You Share Your Video

Send us your long-form content from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or any other platform. You can share the video link or upload the file directly.

Include any specific details about which sections you'd like us to focus on, or let us find the best moments for maximum engagement.

2

We Create Your Sample

Our team gets to work creating a sample clip from your video. We add professional captions, optimize for vertical format (9:16), and include your branding.

You'll receive your sample within 48 hours via WhatsApp, Telegram, or email—whichever you prefer.

3

You Review & Approve

Check out the sample clip and let us know what you think. If you want revisions, we'll make them within 24 hours at no extra cost.

This is your chance to see our work quality and editing style before committing to a subscription.

4

Payment & Subscription Begins

Once you're happy with the sample, we'll send you payment details. We accept cryptocurrency, Wise transfers, Ko-fi donations, and more.

After payment, your subscription starts and you'll begin receiving your monthly clips on schedule.

5

You Post & Grow

Download your ready-to-post clips and share them across all your platforms. Watch your reach and engagement explode.

We'll keep delivering high-quality clips every month, and you'll keep growing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What video formats do you accept?

We accept videos from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and any standard video format. Just share the link or upload the file.

How long does the sample process take?

We guarantee delivery within 48 hours. Most samples are delivered within 24-36 hours.

Can I request revisions to the sample?

Absolutely! We'll revise the sample within 24 hours at no extra cost. You can request changes to captions, music, pacing, or any other aspect.

What happens after I approve the sample?

We'll send you payment details via your preferred platform (WhatsApp, Telegram, or email). Once payment is received, your subscription begins immediately.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Just let us know, and we'll stop creating clips for you.

What if I'm not satisfied with the sample?

If you're not happy with the sample after revisions, there's no obligation to purchase. We want you to be 100% confident in our work before committing.
