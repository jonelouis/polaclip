Frequently Asked Questions

What's the best way to contact you?

WhatsApp and Telegram are fastest for immediate responses. Email works too if you prefer a more formal channel.

How quickly will you respond?

We typically respond within 1-2 hours on WhatsApp and Telegram, and within 24 hours on email.

Can I request a sample through any of these channels?

Yes! You can request a sample through WhatsApp, Telegram, or email. Just let us know about your video and we'll get started.

Do you offer support after I become a client?

Absolutely. We provide ongoing support through all these channels. Any questions or issues, just reach out.

What information should I provide when contacting you?

Share your video link (YouTube, Facebook, etc.), the platform you want clips for (Reels, Shorts, TikTok), and any specific preferences you have.

Is there a contact form on this website?

We prefer direct communication through WhatsApp, Telegram, or email for faster responses. These channels are more reliable than contact forms.
